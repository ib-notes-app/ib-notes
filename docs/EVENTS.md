# Record an event and review its day

[Back to IB Notes](../README.md) · [Getting started](QUICK_START.md) · [Sharing](SHARING.md)

Use Events for releases and other short windows you want to study: CPI, PPI,
FOMC, earnings or your own label. You enter the information yourself. IB Notes
does not fetch or verify a live news calendar.

## Add an event while recording

1. Open **Record price → + Event**. Your typed price stays in its draft.
2. Choose **Add event**, enter a clear name and check its date and release time.
3. Choose how long to track: 1, 5, 15 or 30 minutes, a custom window, or ongoing.
4. Add an event note if useful, then **Save event**.

The release time and tracking window can differ. For example, you can start
tracking before the release. Open **Adjust tracking window** for those controls.
An ongoing window can be ended with **End now**. Check your configured time zone.
Windows that span two dates show both dates on shared cards.
Opening Events does not record a price or reopen a finalized day.

## What gets tracked?

CP observations with times inside the window contribute to its **recorded high,
low, midpoint, width and timed-price count**. Backdated prices use their
observation time, not the moment you saved them. High/Low entries use a known-by
time and do not invent a precise occurrence inside an event window.

You are seeing your recorded sample. A quiet window or one price is not complete
market coverage. Overlapping events may include the same observation; the app
does not claim either event caused the move.

Use **Add Note** to link a note to an event when useful. Structure also shows
**Event windows**, where you can inspect the observed range and linked notes.

## Find CPI days, PPI days or another event

Open **Review → Days → Event days** and select the event name or category.
You can open the matching sessions and inspect their IB. In **IB stats**, the
event-day filter lets you compare the recorded outcomes for that group.

A day with CPI and Core CPI is counted once in the matching day group. Check the
number of eligible days; missing IB outcomes stay unknown. Your manually entered
labels and records determine what is found, and an event filter does not supply
missing forecasts or market history.

## Include events on a share card

From a period's **Share this period** screen, choose **News & events** if you
want the event information included. Available recorded event ranges can appear
with it. Check the preview: event notes and linked private prose are not swept
into that option automatically. Your journal and notes remain local unless you
explicitly export or share them.

An election or another ongoing situation can be event context without a single
numerical result. The card does not call that a missing release figure. Recording
an event alongside volatility does not establish that it caused the movement.
