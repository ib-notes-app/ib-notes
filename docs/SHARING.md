# Make a share card

[Back to IB Notes](../README.md) · [Read the dashboard](READING_THE_DASHBOARD.md) · [Events](EVENTS.md)

## Start with the period

1. On Home, tap **ON, 15R, A, B or RTH**.
2. Choose **Share this period**. You do not need to edit or save the range first.
3. **Image Card** opens by default. Choose the **Sharing period** you want,
   including the full-session option.
4. Pick the information you want and check the preview.
5. Choose **Download PNG**, or use the available copy/share action yourself.

ON shows the overnight structure. Each later period adds the earlier recorded
structure: 15R includes ON, A includes ON and 15R, and so on. Earlier selections
do not show later-period prices as if they were already known. RTH can include
the full regular-session range; the full-session choice shows the session so far.
A missing range stays missing.

## Keep your usual card setup

Your content switches, map options and display choices are remembered in that
browser until you change them. You do not need to build the same layout for each
post. Notes and nearby levels still require individual selection for the day;
private selections from another date are not carried into a new card.

Recap, forecast, structure and conditions are choices on the same card. Nearby
levels add detail to it rather than create a separate kind of card. Open
**More details** for the other map, evidence and time-zone options.

If the card fills up, additions that would overflow it become dimmed. Remove
an item to make space. Already selected items stay removable. The app does not
quietly drop details or shrink everything into unreadable text.

The preview uses the image's full height. Scroll to its final rows and footer;
they are part of the downloaded PNG too.

## What each part adds

- **Structure map:** the recorded path and range references, with labeled
  midpoints. A midpoint stays on the map rather than becoming a carried level.
- **Recap:** recorded ranges, widths, break/return totals and suitable historical
  comparisons. A gold width callout needs enough compatible earlier records;
  its evidence names the source and sample size.
- **Forecast:** the existing estimate, its target and its time. ON uses opening
  bands; the IB periods use outcome bars. After the session, suitable saved
  forecasts can be shown beside recorded results.
- **Current conditions:** the latest recorded price, its time and position on
  the viewed range. It is a recorded observation, not a live quote feed.
- **Nearby levels:** selected references with above/below/inside labels and
  distance from the recorded price. A reference called Low can be above price.
- **Your notes, News & events, Commentary:** only the material you select or
  write. Commentary starts blank rather than repeating the card's facts.

Historical width comparisons have no fixed day-count limit. They use eligible
earlier records from the named source. Your journal and learning history stay
separate; the card prefers your journal when available. A range-comparison count
is different from the similar cases used in a forecast.

## Read the overnight move

With **Recap** selected, **Recorded ON move** shows the change between your
first and latest recorded overnight CP. The graphic includes both prices and
their times. It needs two clear observations; a single quote cannot establish
a change. Equal first/latest prices correctly show zero change.

This is the move between your entries, not a return from the market open or
proof of what happened between them. Your actual recorded price path remains
on the Structure map. Turning Recap off removes this graphic.

## Read the forecast on the card

The card uses the same stage summary and estimate wording as Forecast. Check its
timestamp: another price or a later snapshot can legitimately change the estimate.
Before an edge breaks, the four outcomes include Neither. First-break odds stay
in the optional details so they do not look like a second step in a prediction.

After you review the final daily range, a day with neither edge broken shows
**IBN** in its heading. You do not need a separate path-complete confirmation.
Missing final ranges still leave the result unknown.

After close, Forecast can show the latest saved RTH estimate alongside the
recorded outcome, including IBN. An old estimate keeps its actual percentage;
the app does not rewrite it to match what later happened. New live estimates
can be saved even if no original 15R forecast was available. Missing older
estimates cannot be reconstructed afterward.

**Today in context** describes recorded ON/IB size and location. Historical
comparisons appear only when suitable records exist, with their source and
counts. A past frequency and today's weighted model estimate answer different
questions; they are labeled separately.

A return forecast appears after a recorded break while price is outside. Inside
again, recorded return counts show what happened. An unbroken session does not
need an “Already inside” forecast row. Events such as elections can provide
context without a single numerical result or a claim that they caused a move.

**Short Post** and **Full Recap** remain available if you want text. Weeks,
months and years have their own recap sharing screens with Image Card first.

## Share a week, month or year

Open **Review**, choose the time span, then **Share week**, **Share month** or
**Share year**. Image Card opens first, with the same visual language as session
cards and statistics for the larger time span.

The headline identifies the most common recorded IB outcome, including ties.
The card shows outcome frequencies, narrowest/typical/widest IB sizes, returns
after a recorded break, midpoint returns and closing locations. The breakdown
compares days within a week, weeks within a month, or months within a year.
Width bars compare range sizes rather than raw price levels.

Each statistic keeps its own denominator. For example, recorded return days use
days with a recorded first break; closing locations use known closes. Missing
outcomes do not count as Neither, and future plans do not count as results.

When comparable saved IB-lock forecasts exist, the card can show how often the
recorded result was among their leading outcomes. Ties are identified. This is
a reviewed count, not a new forecast or a promise of accuracy. Notes remain
individually selectable, and Short Post and Full Recap remain available.

## Before posting

Every image card includes **Not financial advice or trade signals**. The period
cards also explain: **Recorded observations: human error or gaps between entries
may leave price movements unrecorded.** This is a reminder that your entries
show what you recorded, rather than every market movement. Replay and other
relevant qualifications remain visible without repeating the same notice.

Check the date, period, prices, event times and any selected notes. An unavailable
forecast stays unavailable; exporting does not create odds. A return count means
recorded returns, not proof of every move between your observations.

Downloading or copying creates your chosen output. It does not automatically
post to X or another service. Confirm you are allowed to share the underlying
data and any derived content; the app license grants no market-data rights.
See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md) and the [data-rights reminder](CSV_IMPORT.md#check-your-data-rights-first).

After an app update, export a fresh image. Downloaded files and existing posts
keep the wording they had when you made them.

Structure journal only · No trade recommendations.
