# What's new

## IBN results and an evolving no-break outlook

A reviewed day that stayed inside the IB now shows **IBN** on its closing card.
The final daily high and low establish that result; there is no extra
path-complete checkbox to find.

Before either edge breaks, suitable learning history can now account for how
late the session has remained unbroken, with comparable IB widths. Without
compatible minute-price history, the app labels the full-session baseline.
No market history is bundled, and these estimates remain experimental.

Live estimates can be saved even when an original 15R forecast was unavailable.
The closing card uses the latest saved RTH estimate beside the recorded result.
Past forecasts keep their original values and times. A locked IB width is a
recorded fact, rather than a new width estimate.


## Clearer sharing cards

The overnight image no longer repeats its width statistic in an unfinished
sentence. With **Recap** selected, it can instead show a **Recorded ON move**
graphic: the point change from your first recorded overnight price to your
latest, with both prices and times. It waits for two clear observations and
does not claim to measure the move from the market open.

Comparison bars now name the earlier ON or IB ranges they count. Their text
wraps cleanly above the bars. Weekly, monthly and annual footer notes use
complete sentences. The recorded path, forecasts and text formats are unchanged.


## Your last saved price stays visible

Record price now shows **Last price entered and saved** with the saved CP
below Entries, Add Note, Event and Details. It remains there when you close
and reopen the sheet or reload the app, until another CP is successfully saved.
Typing, canceling, a failed save or a note without a price does not replace it.
Each day keeps its own value.

If you save a backdated price, this receipt names that latest save. The
dashboard still uses observation time to show the latest recorded position.


## Clearer history limits on forecasts

When few learning sessions have an IB range as wide or narrow as yours, the
live RTH forecast shows a short **Limited range history** message. The same
message appears on an image card when Forecast is selected. It describes how
much comparable range history is available; it is not another probability.

The odds calculation has not changed. A rare range is a reason to be careful
with an estimate, not proof that either edge will or will not break. No learning
history is bundled with the app, and missing history still means Unavailable.

## Easier period range entry

High and Low now come first when you edit ON, 15R, A, B or RTH. Save/Confirm
and Close stay above the scrolling content, with the period map below the
range fields. This reduces searching around when the keyboard opens. Actual
keyboard behavior still depends on the phone and browser.

## Clearer cards for every part of the session

Image Cards now share one look from ON through RTH: a bold outcome or period
heading, aqua lows, pink highs, compact range tiles and a plum forecast panel.
Your selected sections control what appears. Removing a section also removes
any highlight that depended on it.

The map labels its midpoints and prices. **Recap** contains recorded range
sizes and comparisons with earlier history. **Current conditions** shows the
latest recorded price on a small range ruler. Forecasts keep their target and
estimate time together, and saved estimates can be compared with later results.
Earlier snapshots keep their own counts instead of borrowing closing totals.

Nearby references show whether they are above, below or around the recorded
price. Notes are easier to read, and event ranges use the same low/high/midpoint
colors. The full-session card keeps recorded break and return totals.

## Reviews tell the bigger story

Weekly, monthly and annual cards now use the same visual style with broader
statistics: which IB outcomes occurred, typical range sizes, recorded returns
after breaks and where sessions closed relative to IB.

Weekly cards compare days, monthly cards compare weeks, and annual cards compare
months. Counts show what they are based on, and unknown outcomes stay separate.
When comparable saved forecasts exist, a reviewed count shows whether their
leading outcomes included the recorded result. Tied estimates are labeled.
These are summaries of your records, not predictions for the next week or year.

Selected notes still require your choice. Check the complete preview before
downloading; an overfull selection blocks export instead of shrinking the text.

## Forecast and sharing tell the same story

Forecast and image cards now use the same summary for the stage of the day,
with the estimate time shown. Before an IB edge breaks, all four outcomes stay
visible: High only, Low only, Both and Neither. First-break odds are a separate
question under Details, rather than a second headline that looks like a sequence.

**Today in context** explains the recorded ON and IB sizes and where the IB sits
within ON. When suitable earlier records exist, it also shows range comparisons
and historical counts. Your journal and learning history stay separate. A past
frequency is labeled separately from today's weighted model estimate.

Return forecasts wait for a recorded edge break and an outside price. Once price
is back inside, recorded return counts provide the context. There is no
“Already inside” forecast placeholder before anything has broken.

## See your whole image before sharing

The preview now follows the full height of the image. Scroll through it to check
the final rows and footer before **Download PNG**. Prices, widths and midpoints
use your instrument's price step, without long trails of decimals.

Events such as elections no longer appear to need a single numerical result.
Event tracking windows that span two dates show both dates. Numerical release
figures remain available when relevant; event notes remain private unless selected.

## Check a period when it ends

At a configured period end, the app offers its recorded high and low for
confirmation. It waits while an entry or another screen is open. Once confirmed,
the period shows **Saved · Closed · Confirmed** and has an explicit correction
action. Missing prices stay missing; the clock does not invent a range.

Closing an earlier period on the current session returns to the active period.
Historical browsing keeps your selected period. Your journal records, original
forecasts and forecast weights are preserved.

## Your day's structure stays on the map

The selected day's own ON, 15R, A, B/IB and RTH ranges always remain visible
in Structure. Clearing extra map references cannot remove those boundaries,
including when an older version saved them as hidden.

The current period also has a named midpoint on the map and between the low
and high in Current conditions. Both use the same recorded range. It is not
saved as a level or carried into another day. Empty sessions identify their
phase without inventing a range.

## Clear enabled map references in one place

**Levels → Map levels** lists your enabled references with a quick **Hide**
button on each row. See which ones are shown now and which are enabled outside
the map distance. Hiding keeps the record and its history saved.

**Structure → Manage map levels**, or a listed reference under Other map
levels, opens that same section. Back returns to Structure. Optional references
stay hidden after returning or reloading; the day's own boundaries stay visible.
[Read the map-level guide](LEVELS.md#clear-map-references-quickly).

## Clearer share-card footers and phase names

Every image card now says **Not financial advice or trade signals**, including
cards that show only recorded prices or structure. The recording notice appears
once and explains that human error or gaps between entries can leave price
movements unrecorded. Longer warnings stay readable, and replay, source and
level qualifications are kept when relevant.

**Record price → Details** now uses the same **ON, 15R, A, B and RTH** names as
the dashboard. Your entries still save to the selected phase.

After updating, download a new image to get the new footer. Images you already
saved or posted do not change.

## Level confirmations clear themselves

Messages such as **Level enabled on map** and **Level hidden from map** now
disappear after a few seconds and clear when you leave the page or day. Level
note, archive/restore, proximity and recorded-visit confirmations behave the
same way. The saved changes remain in your journal; only the message clears.
A save error stays visible so it is not mistaken for a successful change.

## Levels stay useful through large moves

Home now finds the closest two saved references on each side of recorded CP,
even when they are beyond your map-distance setting. Older contract references
show their month and year with an amber **Prior** badge. Details keep the exact
source contract. Original prices are preserved, and different contract months
stay separate. [Read the level guide](LEVELS.md#levels-from-an-older-contract).

No CSVs, data packs or market history are included. These labels describe your
own saved or permitted imported references; they do not supply a history library.

## Recording is quicker

The entry row now puts **price · SAVE · time** together. Tap the time to change
when the observation happened. The current-session clock keeps updating while
the sheet is open. After a backdated CP save, it returns to now for your next
entry. High, CP and Low have a blue selection outline, leaving pink for SAVE.
The sheet stays open for repeated entries.

**Entries · Add Note · + Event · Details** sit together beneath the field.
Notes open a proper writing page. Save a note with a price and time, or save a
timestamped note on its own. The entry correction screen is more compact too.

## Events have their own recorded windows

Add CPI, PPI, FOMC or another event from Record price. Set its release time,
tracking window and note. Timed CP observations build its recorded high, low,
midpoint and width. Structure shows event windows; Review can find event days
and show their recorded IB statistics. [How Events work](EVENTS.md).

## Sharing follows the session

Tap a period, then **Share this period**. Pick ON, 15R, A, B, RTH or the full
session and combine the details on one image. Your usual card options stay
selected. Options dim when there isn't room for another readable item.
[Make a share card](SHARING.md).

## Missing measurements are easier to find

Amber calendar and period markers lead to the ranges that need attention.
Save the repair in that period; no extra trip to RTH is needed just to confirm
it again. High/low-only days can be complete. Weekly Review shortcuts are in
the calendar, with weekend dates kept for instruments that study weekends.

## A simpler Home menu

The **⋯** menu now opens **Instrument, Time zone, Import & data, Trash,
Backups & storage, and Help** directly. The instrument is shown on Record price.
Events are in the recording sheet. Trash counts stay on Trash instead of
lingering on the dashboard after you leave.

## Getting the update

Save your work and export a SQLite backup from **⋯ → Backups & storage**.
Open your usual app address while online. When **Update safely** appears,
choose it after saving any open draft. Your journal stays in the same browser;
there is no need to import it again or reinstall the app.

If the update isn't offered yet, close and reopen the app while connected.
Check **⋯ → Help → Build** against [the published build](../release-info.json).
Do not clear website data to force an update.

The public journal still starts empty. No market history, personal journals or
news feed are bundled. Forecasts still need suitable permitted learning history.
