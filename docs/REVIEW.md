# Public version — test status

[Open IB Notes](https://ib-notes-app.github.io/ib-notes/) · [What changed](WHATS_NEW.md)

This update recognizes reviewed IBN closes, improves the unbroken-session
outlook when compatible timing history exists, and retains saved RTH forecasts
for closing cards. Past estimates keep their actual values.

The owner accepted the phone version before this public release. The clean
public source build passed. Public packaging checks are recorded in
[release-info.json](../release-info.json). Estimates remain experimental;
automated checks do not establish calibrated prediction accuracy.

This app includes no market history, CSVs, packs, journals, backups, examples
or data-bearing screenshots. Your own permitted data stays on your device.

Save current input, keep a backup, and choose **Update safely** at your usual
app address when offered. Do not clear browser data to force an update.
[Update help](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
